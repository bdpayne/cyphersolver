# Bouillé 1778: the governor of the Windward Islands to the minister of the Navy, Martinique, 17 Sept and 5 Dec 1778

Status: read (Brice Dudout, 28 Oct 2026; 257 of 260 words read in their sense, 237 certain, 16 probable, 4 restored, 3 unresolved; Daniel Bourdeau's 95% rule). The second return attached to no. 259 (image 0164) is not yet read.

## Documents

Archives nationales d’outre-mer (Aix-en-Provence), COL C8 A 77, fol. 88 (image FRANOM23_C8A_077_0141) and fol. 101 (images 0162–0163); IREL records: “Lettre chiffrée non décryptée”; “Lettre et pièces jointes chiffrées non décryptées”.
17 September 1778 (no. 238, duplicate) and 5 December 1778 (no. 259, with a return dated 9 December). Fort-Royal (Martinique) → Versailles.
Sender: François-Claude-Amour, marquis de Bouillé, governor general of the Windward Islands. Recipient: Antoine de Sartine, Secretary of State for the Navy.
Images not included (IREL, no reuse licence found).

Ten days after taking Dominica from the British (7 September 1778), the governor general of the Windward Islands writes in cipher that he lacks men to attack other islands; in December he sends the ciphered return of the new garrison. Both letters are marked “not decrypted” in the inventory of the Archives nationales d’outre-mer. They can be read with Bouillé’s cipher table, rebuilt from five other letters of 1779 deciphered in pencil between the lines.

## System and key

Bouillé’s cipher, 1778–1782: a numerical nomenclator of about 850 numbers, syllabic, with homophones (361 = de, 250 = l’, 607 = le, 817 = s) and code names (267 Martinique, 428 Guadeloupe, 138 Dominica). Different from the table of the minister’s dispatches.

Key built only from the pencil glosses of Bouillé's 1779 letters (ANOM COL C8 A 78: fol. 3 no. 173 and no. 175 with its return; fol. 31 no. 281; fol. 144 no. 372 pages 1-3), 2,352 groups; frozen as K4 (379 values, SHA-256 85adc39bc47c4b3672ee9c1e10cb917f5066fbe3fe9083cc3a26cade73e94a06). An archivist's pencil note on C8 A 78 fol. 3 says these letters were deciphered "thanks to the key at the beginning of register Marine B4 195" (Archives nationales); that period table has not been consulted yet.

## Validation

Held-out glossed letter: C8 A 78 fol. 123, no. 363, 11 Oct 1779 (104 groups). Frozen K4: 0.865 of words and 0.850 of characters reproduced exactly; 200 control keys (values permuted): max 0.038 and 0.165. Criterion fixed in advance: >= 0.85 on both. Two earlier validations with less material failed (0.710 and 0.840 of characters) and are kept in our records.

## Notes

- Context. Bouillé took Dominica on 7 September 1778; the letter of 17 September explains why he went no further: he was 817 men short, and every island taken needed a garrison. The return of 9 December shows how that garrison was made up: line troops, Bouillé’s Volunteers (a corps raised in the islands), militia and men of colour. Subtotals and total check: 26 + 13 = 39; 64 + 81 + 67 = 222; 104 + 4 + 280 + 197 + 145 + 81 + 39 + 222 = 1,072.
- Probable words {x}, with justification. {impatience}: d’+667.665.ti.en.ce; 665 = pa (“cam·pa·gne”, 1779); 667 = im not attested elsewhere, the only possible word. {vuide}: vu+568; 568 not attested; “le vuide de 817 hommes” (period spelling of “vide”). {entreprendre}: d’+146.pren.dre; 146 not attested; “entreprendre sur quelques îles” is the military idiom of the time. {effectifs}: 594.538.345.ti.f.s; 594 = ef and 345 = c are glossed (no. 363: “ef·fe·c·ti·ve·ment”); 538 is otherwise “sept”: perhaps a clerk’s slip for 658 = fe. {soit} (×2): 819, not glossed; same number in the return, where 819.73.ante = soixante is forced by the sums; “soit en troupes réglées, soit en milices”. {réglées}: ré.26.ées, 26 = gl not attested. {J’ai}: 208, not attested; the opening formula is in clear in no. 238. {travaux}: 577.va.u.x, 577 = tra not attested. {Armagnac}: ar.ma.610.c, 610 not attested; a regiment name. {Auxerrois}: au.x.218, 218 = rois not attested. {Bouillé}: 189.il.le, 189 = bou not attested; Bouillé’s Volunteers are known. {couleur}: co.108.leur, 108 = u not attested. {soixante} (×2): 819.73.ante, forced by the sums.
- Words restored [x], with justification. [ministre]: “le 495.re de la guerre m’a annoncées”; the only possible reading (recruits came under the War department). [sans]: 481, not attested; “ne pouvant y mettre une garnison 481 dégarnir la Martinique”; 431 = sans is glossed three times, 481 would be a homophone. [fin]: “approchent de leur 678”. [Gens]: “701 de couleur”.
